Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23/08/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dono ("Dono", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address and password (used for authentication; passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text)
  • Username, bio, and profile photo

Content you create

  • Videos, captions, categories, and audio you upload
  • Comments, likes, and follows
  • Direct messages you send to other users

Financial information

  • Your coin wallet balance and transaction history (donations sent and received)
  • Payout requests you submit
  • If you connect a payout account, limited account status information from our payment processor (see Section 4) — we never see or store your full bank details or card numbers; these are handled directly by Stripe

Device and usage information

  • A push notification token, if you grant permission, used to deliver notifications (e.g. new messages)
  • Basic technical data such as app version and general usage logs, collected automatically through our backend and infrastructure providers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's features (feed, uploads, messaging, donations, payouts)
  • To process donations and payout requests
  • To send you notifications you've opted into
  • To detect, prevent, and act on fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service
  • To respond to support requests and enforce blocks/reports between users

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • With other users — your username, profile photo, bio, and public content (videos, comments, likes) are visible to other users of the App. Messages are visible only to you and the recipient.
  • Service providers — we use third-party infrastructure providers to operate the App:
    • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage for the App
    • Stripe / Stripe Connect — processes creator payouts and handles identity verification (KYC) for anyone requesting a payout, under Stripe's own privacy policy
    • Expo — delivers push notifications on our behalf
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Dono or our users

4. Payments and Payouts

Coin purchases and creator payouts are processed by Stripe. When you request a payout, Stripe may require you to verify your identity and provide banking details directly to Stripe — we do not store this information ourselves. Coins have no cash value outside of the App and are not guaranteed to be exchangeable for real currency except through the payout process described in our Terms of Service.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App's services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 6).

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & correction — you can view and edit most of your account information directly in the App (Edit Profile).
  • Deletion — you may request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us at agive682@gmail.com. Some information may be retained where required for legal, financial record-keeping, or fraud-prevention purposes (e.g. transaction records).
  • Notifications — you can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings.

If you are in the UK, EU, or California, you may have additional rights under applicable law (such as the UK/EU GDPR or the CCPA), including the right to request a copy of your data or object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

Dono is not directed at, and may not be used by, anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it and terminate the associated account.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encryption in transit, access controls, and row-level security on our database — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including wherever our service providers (Supabase, Stripe, Expo) operate their infrastructure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact Us
